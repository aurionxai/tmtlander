The Money Team Legal Network, LLC, including its affiliated entities and related organizations (collectively referred to as "TMT," "we," "us," or "our"), is committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the types of personal information we gather, how we use and protect that information, with whom we may share it, and what options you have regarding our handling of your data.

This Privacy Policy governs personal information collected through our websites at www.themoneyteam.legal and www.themoneyteamlawfirm.com, any current or future mobile applications we operate, our email and electronic communications, our social media presence, and any other digital or wireless channels we maintain that link to this Policy (collectively, the "Platform"). It also applies to personal information gathered in connection with the legal and related services we provide.

1. Information We Collect

The personal information we collect varies depending on the services you request and, where you are an active client, the nature of your legal matter. The categories below describe the types of personal information we may gather. We may also collect additional information not specifically listed here, which will be handled consistent with this Privacy Policy or as disclosed at the point of collection.

Certain categories above — including government-issued identifiers, financial data, medical information, and precise geolocation — may be treated as "sensitive" personal information under applicable state privacy laws. Additional rights regarding such information are described in Section 7 below.

A. Information You Provide Directly

We collect personal information that you voluntarily submit when using our Platform — for example, when you request a case evaluation, create or access an account, complete intake forms, or otherwise communicate with us. Where our Platform is used by active clients to communicate within an attorney-client relationship, those communications are handled with appropriate confidentiality protections. Providing the requested information is generally optional; however, declining to do so may limit our ability to serve you.

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you interact with our Platform, we and our technology partners may automatically collect certain technical and usage data using the following tools:

Cookies — Small data files stored by your browser or mobile device that help us remember your preferences, personalize your experience, support security functions, and facilitate advertising. You may adjust cookie settings through your browser's options menu or by visiting https://allaboutcookies.org. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features.

Web Beacons / Tracking Pixels — Transparent image files embedded in web pages or emails that help us understand whether content was viewed or links were accessed, and to measure the effectiveness of our communications.

Software Development Kits (SDKs) — Third-party code integrated into our mobile application to support analytics, social media features, and advertising functions. SDKs may permit third parties to collect data directly through the App.

Session Recording Technologies — Tools that capture video replays of user interactions with our Site — including clicks, scrolling, and typing — for the purpose of improving usability, troubleshooting technical issues, and enhancing the overall experience.

Local Storage — Browser-based data storage used to remember your preferences, support navigation, and measure the relevance of content displayed to you.

Chat and Messaging Tools — Interactive features on our Site, including automated chat assistants, that allow you to communicate with us or receive answers to inquiries. These tools may record chat content and collect usage data for service improvement purposes.

C. Information Received from Third Parties

We may also obtain personal information from outside sources, including: business partners, data vendors, and advertising service providers; publicly available records and social media platforms; third parties involved in your legal matter, such as healthcare providers, opposing parties, or other individuals you have authorized to share information with us; and referral sources, including members of our referral network or individuals who submit your contact information through our website or other channels.

Information received from third parties may be combined with information we collect from you directly or automatically.

2. Analytics and Tracking Tools

We use third-party analytics platforms — such as Google Analytics — to better understand how visitors engage with our Platform and to identify opportunities for improvement. These services collect information transmitted by your browser or device, including pages visited and navigation patterns.

To learn how Google collects, uses, and shares information, please visit https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. To opt out of Google Analytics tracking, you may install the browser add-on available at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes, as well as for any additional purposes described in this Privacy Policy or disclosed at the time of collection:

To Deliver Our Services. Evaluate your potential legal matter and identify your needs; provide legal representation, case management, and related client services; create, administer, and secure your client account; respond to your questions and communicate with you, including by phone, email, or text (which may involve automated messaging or prerecorded content); process invoices, billing, and settlement distributions; personalize your experience and deliver relevant content; and maintain and improve the performance and security of our Platform.

Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies. In operating our business, we may use tools and services powered by artificial intelligence or machine learning. These technologies may support marketing operations, content development, workflow efficiency, case management support, or client service functions. Where such tools interact with or process your personal information, that information remains subject to this Privacy Policy unless a separate supplemental notice is provided.

Direct and Informational Communications. We may send you legal updates, newsletters, event announcements, or other communications we believe may be of interest. You may opt out of marketing messages at any time as described in Section 5.

Research and Platform Improvement. We analyze usage patterns and aggregate data to improve our Platform, develop new features, diagnose technical problems, and understand how our services are used. We may create anonymized or de-identified datasets from personal information for these purposes and share such data with partners where appropriate.

Legal Compliance and Risk Management. Comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, and legal obligations; enforce our Terms of Use and other agreements; protect the rights, safety, and property of our firm, our clients, and third parties; detect, prevent, and investigate fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity; and conduct internal audits for compliance and quality assurance purposes.

Targeted and Interest-Based Advertising. We, along with our service providers and advertising partners, may use certain personal information for targeted advertising — also referred to as cross-context behavioral advertising under some state laws. This involves working with third-party and social media advertising platforms to display ads we believe may be relevant to you, based on data collected across our Platform and other sites. You may exercise opt-out rights as described in Section 5.

4. How We Share Your Information

We may share personal information with the following categories of parties, in addition to any specific disclosures described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy:

Technology and Service Providers. We engage third-party vendors who perform functions on our behalf — including web hosting, data analytics, email and SMS delivery, event platforms, and IT services. These providers access personal information only to the extent necessary to fulfill their designated functions and are not permitted to use it for independent purposes. Mobile phone numbers and related messaging data will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes, but may be disclosed to our messaging service providers or as required by law.

Co-Counsel and Referral Partners. With your direction or consent, we may share your information with other law firms or attorneys in connection with joint representation, case referrals, or as otherwise required in the course of your legal matter. Information shared within an attorney-client relationship will be handled with all applicable confidentiality protections.

Legal and Regulatory Authorities. We may disclose personal information to comply with applicable laws, respond to valid legal process, protect the rights or safety of individuals, enforce our agreements, or cooperate with law enforcement. If you are a client or party in a matter we handle, certain information may be disclosed to courts or regulatory bodies as required.

Corporate Transactions. In connection with a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, financing, reorganization, or similar business transaction (including in bankruptcy proceedings), we may transfer personal information to relevant parties as part of the transaction.

Advertising and Marketing Partners. We may share personal information with third-party advertising or joint marketing partners for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Mobile phone numbers will not be shared with advertising partners for promotional use.

Professional Advisors. We may share personal information with our legal counsel, financial advisors, auditors, and insurers to the extent necessary in the course of professional services they render to us.

Affiliated Entities. We may share information with current or future affiliated companies that are under common control with TMT, for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

With Your Consent. We may share your personal information in other circumstances where you have provided explicit authorization, or where required or permitted by applicable law.

5. Your Privacy Choices

Marketing and Promotional Communications. To stop receiving marketing emails, contact us at privacy@themoneyteam.legal or write to us at the address in Section 9 below. You may also use the unsubscribe link included in any marketing email. Please allow up to ten (10) business days to process your request. We may continue to send transactional or service-related communications even if you opt out of marketing.

Text Messages (SMS). If you receive SMS messages from us, you may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts from a particular short code. A confirmation message may follow. Standard messaging and data rates may apply. You may also submit an opt-out request by emailing privacy@themoneyteam.legal with your name and mobile number.

Cookies and Browser Tracking. You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that limiting cookies may affect the availability or performance of certain Platform features. For more information about cookies, including how to view and remove them, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

Interest-Based Advertising. Members of the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) and the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) offer opt-out mechanisms at http://www.networkadvertising.org/managing/opt_out.asp and optout.aboutads.info. You may also use browser privacy extensions or adjust your mobile device settings to restrict advertising identifiers. Individual advertising platforms offer their own preference controls. Note that opt-out settings are device- and browser-specific and must be set on each device you use. Opting out of interest-based advertising does not eliminate online advertising; you will simply see ads that are less tailored to your interests.

"Do Not Track" Signals. At this time, our Platform does not alter its data collection practices in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals. For more information, visit http://www.allaboutdnt.com.

Declining to Provide Information. Providing personal information is voluntary in most cases; however, certain services cannot be delivered without it. If you choose not to provide requested information, we may be unable to fulfill the relevant service request.

C. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence, and subject to applicable limitations and exceptions, you may have the following rights: Right to Know — to confirm whether we process personal information about you and, in some cases, to obtain information about the categories collected, sources, purposes, and parties with whom it has been shared or sold. Right to Access and Portability — to request access to the specific personal information we hold about you and, where required by law, to receive a copy in a portable and usable format. Right to Correction — to request that we correct inaccurate information we maintain about you. Right to Deletion — to request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions. Right to Opt Out of Sales — to request that we stop "selling" your personal information as defined under applicable state law. Right to Opt Out of Targeted Advertising — to request that we stop using your personal information for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising. Right to Revoke Consent or Limit Sensitive Data Use — to withdraw consent to our processing of sensitive personal information, or to request that we restrict such processing to permissible purposes. Right to Non-Discrimination — to exercise the rights above without retaliation. Note that exercising certain rights (such as deletion) may affect our ability to provide services.
