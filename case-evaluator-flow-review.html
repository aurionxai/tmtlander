1  Auto Accident

AUTO-Q1"When did the accident happen?"
  • Within last 30 days / 31–60 / 61–90 days → Continue to AUTO-FAULT
  • 90 days – 1 year → AUTO-Q1a (medical-bills sub-gate)
  • More than 1 year ago → AUTO-Q1b (severity sub-gate)
AUTO-Q1a(90 days–1 yr) "Do you have big medical bills … like $10,000 or more?"
  • Yes — $10k+ or Not sure of amount → Continue to AUTO-FAULT
  • Yes but under $10k or No big bills yet → ask "Are you still going to the doctor / PT / specialist?"
    • Yes — still treating → Continue to AUTO-FAULT
    • Treatment ended / Just minor / one-time care → Disqualified Accidents past 90 days usually need $10,000+ in medical bills or active ongoing treatment. This one may not meet that bar for our firm.
AUTO-Q1b(over 1 yr) "Are your injuries permanent or life-changing?"
  • Yes — serious / life-changing → Continue to AUTO-FAULT
  • Significant but improving / Mostly minor → Disqualified Accidents over a year old usually need serious, permanent injuries for us to take on. We recommend calling a local attorney directly to confirm your options.
AUTO-FAULT"Did you cause the accident?"
  • No — other driver caused it / Fault is shared or unclear → Continue to AUTO-INJURY-GATE

AUTO-PASS1(at-fault driver) "Were there any passengers in your car?"
  • No — I was the only one → Disqualified Since you were at fault and were the only person in the car, there generally isn't an injury claim here. If you think fault is actually shared or unclear, call us — we're happy to review it.

AUTO-PASS2"Was a passenger injured in the accident?"

  • Yes — a passenger was injured Even though you were at fault, an injured passenger may still have a case — they could be eligible for substantial financial compensation. 💪 → AUTO-PASS3
AUTO-PASS3"Would you like to refer them for a free, no-obligation consultation?"

  • Yes — refer the passenger → Referral Great — let's get the passenger's contact details so a case manager can reach out right away. There's no cost and no obligation. 💪 Routes to the contact form, tagged passengerReferral:true, referralType:"injured-passenger". 2  Workers' Compensation

WC-Q1"Were you hurt at work or while doing your job?"
  • Yes, hurt at work → Continue to WC-Q2
  • Commuting to/from work → bot notes commuting is "tricky" → Continue to WC-Q2 Commuting is not auto-disqualified — flag for review (most states bar "going & coming").
  • No — not work-related → "Check if this counts as a regular injury case instead?"
    • Yes, check personal injury → switches to the Personal Injury flow (§3)
    • No thanks → Disqualified Workers' comp only covers injuries that happen at work. Since this didn't happen on the job, it won't qualify.
WC-Q2"Does your employer have workers' comp insurance?"
  • Yes / Unsure / No → all Continue to WC-Q3 "No" triggers reassurance ("they can still be held responsible") — never disqualifies.
WC-Q3(time gate) "Less than one year since you got hurt — or since your last workers' comp payment?"
  • Yes — within the last year / Got a payment within the last year (resets clock) → injury multi-select → ATTORNEY GATE (§7)
    If gate passes: Qualified — "✅ It looks like you have a workers' comp claim worth fighting for. 💪" → upload → contact → retainer e-signature.
  • No — over a year → Disqualified Claims over one year old are hard to file, but exceptions may apply. Talk to an attorney before walking away.

6  SSDI / Disability

Note: the SSDI path skips the document-upload and contact-form steps. A qualified SSDI lead goes directly to retainer options (sign now / email / text).

SSDI-Q1"Are you unable to work because of a medical condition?"
  • Yes — can't work / Can only work a little → Continue to SSDI-Q2
  • No — able to work now → Disqualified Disability benefits require that you can't work a regular job. If that changes, reach out — we're here.
SSDI-Q2"Are you already getting disability benefits from Social Security?"
  • No — not getting any → Continue to SSDI-Q3
  • Applied but was denied → bot reassures (60% denied) → Continue to SSDI-Q3 (flagged as prior denial)
  • Yes — already get SSDI/SSI → Disqualified You're already receiving benefits. If you think you're getting less than you deserve or you've been cut off, call us directly.
SSDI-Q3"Are you working and earning money right now?"
  • No / Part-time / very little → Continue to SSDI-Q4
  • Yes — working regularly → SGA check: "Earning more than $1,550/month?"
    • No — under $1,550 → Continue to SSDI-Q4
    • Yes — over $1,550 → Disqualified Since you're earning over $1,550 a month, Social Security will consider you able to work. Reach out when your situation changes. Hard-coded SGA figure ($1,550). Confirm it tracks the current SSA annual amount.
SSDI-Q4"Has your condition lasted at least 12 months — or is it expected to?"
  • Yes 12+ months / Doctors expect it to / Won't get better / life-threatening → Continue to SSDI-Q5
  • No — less than 12 months → Disqualified Your condition needs to last at least 12 months to qualify. Check back when more time has passed — we're here.
SSDI-Q5"Have you worked and paid into Social Security over the years?"
  • Many years / Some years / Only a few years / Never worked → all Continue to SSDI-Q6 "Never worked" / "a few years" are not disqualified — bot pivots to SSI eligibility instead.
SSDI-Q6"Have you ever applied for disability benefits before?" (skipped if already flagged denied)
  • Never / Pending / Denied / Denied 2+ times → all Continue to SSDI-Q7 (different encouragement messages; none disqualify)
SSDI-Q7"Are you married?" (Yes / No / Widowed) → SSDI-Q8 (no branching)
SSDI-Q8Approximate monthly income (thresholds differ married vs. single)
  • Low / Mid / High → all Continue Income never disqualifies — bot states "SSDI itself has no income limit"; income only informs SSI routing.
SSDI-Q9Conditions multi-select → ATTORNEY GATE (§7)
If gate passes: Qualified — message tailored (general "strong candidate" or, if previously denied, "people who appeal with an attorney are 3× more likely to win"). Routes directly to retainer options: Sign now (e-signature modal) / Email me the agreement / Text me the agreement. No upload or contact form.
