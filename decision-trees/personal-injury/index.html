Shared pre-flow + universal knockouts

Every conversation on every product runs through this tree first. Two of the universal knockouts live here — already-hired-attorney and already-settled — so a visitor who's blocked here never reaches the product-specific gates below.

    A([Pick a product type
Auto · PI · WC · NH · MedMal · SSDI]):::go --> B([What's your name?]):::q
    B --> C{{Yourself, or someone else?}}:::q

    C -->|Someone else| D{{Legal rep / guardian,
or friend / family?}}:::q
    D -->|Legal rep / guardian| E
    D -->|Friend / family| E
    E{{Already hired an attorney
OR settled this claim?}}:::q
    E -->|No to both| F([Continue to product questions]):::go
    E -->|Yes — hired an attorney| G([DQ
Switch attorneys first, then come back]):::dq
    E -->|Yes — claim already settled| H([DQ
Cannot reopen a settled claim]):::dq

Personal Injury

Slip & fall, premises, dog bites. Same 30/90 age-bucket pattern as Auto but with a $50k big-bills threshold (higher because the case has to clear a bigger damages bar at that age).
