Shared pre-flow + universal knockouts

Every conversation on every product runs through this tree first. Two of the universal knockouts live here — already-hired-attorney and already-settled — so a visitor who's blocked here never reaches the product-specific gates below.

Product-specific tree

Medical Malpractice

Four sub-paths depending on the type — diagnosis / bed sores / birth injury / medication error. Birth-injury gets an extended 18-year statute window because the child's claim is protected differently under the law.

    Type{{What best describes

    Type -->|Wrong / missed diagnosis| Diag

    Type -->|Birth injury| Birth
    Type -->|Medication error| Med

    Birth{{When did the birth injury occur?}}:::q
    Birth -->|Within the last 3 years| Q2([QUALIFIES]):::go

    Birth -->|More than 18 years ago| DQbirth([DQ
Outside even the extended statute]):::dq

    Med{{How serious were the consequences
