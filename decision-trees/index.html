Shared pre-flow + universal knockouts

🚗 Auto Accident

Driver / passenger / pedestrian role split, fault gate, passenger-referral side channel, insurance / claim / police trunk, then 30-day OR 90-day-to-1-year branch — $10k big-bills gate and soft-only DQ live in the 90-day path.

🏗️ Workers' Comp

On-the-job injuries. The only consumer flow with explicit redirects — commuting pivots to AUTO, not-work-related pivots to PERSONAL INJURY. HARD injury qualifies outright; SOFT-only needs 3+ months off work.

Four sub-paths depending on the type — diagnosis / bed sores / birth injury / medication error. Birth-injury gets an extended 18-year statute window because the child's claim is protected differently under the law.
