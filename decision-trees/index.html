Shared Pre-Flow (every product runs this first)

Asked once at the start of every conversation, regardless of which product the visitor picked. Two of the universal knockouts live here. 

flowchart TD
    A([Pick a product type
Auto · PI · WC · NH · MedMal · SSDI]):::go --> B([What's your name?]):::q
    B --> C{{Yourself, or someone else?}}:::q
    C -->|Yourself| E
    C -->|Someone else| D{{Legal rep / guardian,
or friend / family?}}:::q
    D -->|Legal rep / guardian| E
    D -->|Friend / family| E
    E{{Already hired an attorney
OR settled this claim?}}:::q
    E -->|No to both| F([Continue to product questions]):::go
    E -->|Yes – hired an attorney| G([DQ
Switch attorneys first, then come back]):::dq
    E -->|Yes – claim already settled| H([DQ
Can't reopen a settled claim]):::dq

🚗 Auto Accident

The most-branched tree in the evaluator. Splits three ways on role (driver / passenger / pedestrian), then re-merges before the time-bucket gate.

⚕️ Personal Injury (GPI)

Slip & fall, premises, dog bites. The 90-day-to-1-year branch uses a $50k big-bills threshold (higher than Auto's $10k) because the case has to clear a bigger damages bar at that age.

Outside statute window]):::dq

    Allow -->|Yes| Where{{Where did it happen?

🏗️ Workers' Comp

The only flow with explicit redirects — a "commuting" or "not-work-related" answer doesn't end the chat, it pivots the whole conversation to Auto or GPI without restarting. 

flowchart TD

or somewhere else?}}:::q
    Where -->|Yes – at work| Inc{{Does your employer have
workers' comp insurance?}}:::q
    Where -->|Commuting to / from work| Rauto([Redirect → AUTO
"reviewed as auto accident"]):::redir
    Where -->|Not work-related| Rgpi([Redirect → PERSONAL INJURY]):::redir
    Where -->|No – not hurt| DQno([DQ
Nothing to review]):::dq

    Inc -->|Yes / Unsure / No| Time{{Less than a year since
injury or last WC payment?}}:::q

Outside WC statute]):::dq

🏥 Nursing Home

🩺 Medical Malpractice

Four sub-trees depending on the type of incident. Birth-injury gets an extended 18-year statute window because the child's claim is protected differently under the law. 

flowchart TD
    Start([Medical Malpractice]):::go --> Type{{What best describes
what happened?}}:::q

    Type -->|Wrong / missed diagnosis| Diag
    Type -->|Bed sores| Diag
    Type -->|Birth injury| Birth
    Type -->|Medication error| Med

    Diag{{Would the right diagnosis sooner
OR proper care have meaningfully
changed the outcome?}}:::q
    Diag -->|Yes – big difference| Time1
    Diag -->|Probably / possibly| Time1
    Diag -->|No – outcome would have been the same| DQcause([DQ
No causation, no case]):::dq

    Time1{{Less than a year since it happened
OR you suspected something went wrong?}}:::q
    Time1 -->|Yes| Q1([QUALIFIES]):::go
    Time1 -->|No| DQt1([DQ
Outside statute]):::dq

    Birth{{When did the birth injury occur?}}:::q
    Birth -->|Within the last 3 years| Q2([QUALIFIES]):::go
    Birth -->|Within the last 18 years| Q3([QUALIFIES
child-claim extended statute]):::go
    Birth -->|More than 18 years ago| DQbirth([DQ
Outside even the extended statute]):::dq

    Med{{How serious were the consequences
of the medication error?}}:::q
    Med -->|Death| Q4([QUALIFIES]):::go
    Med -->|Permanent severe impairment / disability| Q5([QUALIFIES]):::go
    Med -->|Other / minor| DQmed([DQ
Case value too low for med-mal economics]):::dq

♿ SSDI / Disability

SSDI uses its own legacy flow (it doesn't share the consumer front-matter). Five sequential knockouts before qualification. 

flowchart TD
    Start([SSDI / Disability]):::go --> Work{{Can you currently work?}}:::q
    Work -->|I cannot work due to medical condition| Cur
    Work -->|I can still work| DQcanwork([DQ
SSDI requires inability to work]):::dq

    Cur{{Are you already receiving
SSDI / SSI benefits?}}:::q
    Cur -->|No / Wrong amount / Was cut off| Now
    Cur -->|Yes – full benefits, all good| DQfull([DQ
Already fully covered]):::dq

    Now{{Are you currently working at all?}}:::q
    Now -->|No| Dur
    Now -->|Yes – a little, part-time| SGA{{Earning over ~$1,550 / month?}}:::q
    SGA -->|Over $1,550| DQsga([DQ
Above substantial-gainful-activity limit]):::dq
    SGA -->|Under $1,550 / Not sure| Dur

    Dur{{Condition lasted or expected
to last 12+ months?}}:::q
    Dur -->|Yes – 12+ months| Cred
    Dur -->|No – under 12 months| DQdur([DQ
SSDI requires 12-month duration]):::dq

    Cred{{Have you worked 5 of the last 10 years
paying into Social Security?}}:::q
    Cred -->|Yes / No / Not sure| Applied
    Applied{{Have you applied for SSDI yet?}}:::q
    Applied -->|Not yet / Pending / Denied / Approved| Conds[+ Married? Income? Conditions multi-pick]:::q
    Conds --> Q([QUALIFIES]):::go
