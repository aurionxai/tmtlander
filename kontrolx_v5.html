kontrolx · Works on any record · V5 · July 2026

Not just leads. Any record with a price.

A lead is only one kind of record. A policy, a claim, a patient referral, a loan application — anywhere a row carries a cost, a revenue, or an obligation, the same engine runs it: intake → screen → enrich → route → settle → serve.

If you can describe what a row is worth, kontrolx can run on it. The verticals below are examples of the same pipeline — not separate products.

One Engine

The pipeline is industry-agnostic

Every vertical maps to the same six stages. What changes is the shape of the record and who it routes to — not the platform underneath.

02ScreenEligibility, dedupe, blacklist, and rule checks at submit.
03EnrichVendor pipelines + human-in-the-loop forms fill what's missing.
04RouteTo the right buyer, carrier, provider, or firm — with accept/return windows.
05SettleDouble-entry ledger: costs, revenue, commissions, statements.
06ServePartner portals, per-audience dashboards, AI on every screen.

In Depth

Insurance & healthcare, on the same rails

Two record-heavy, compliance-sensitive industries — run end to end without a single bolt-on.

Insurance

The record: a quote, policy, or claim

Intake quotes and applications by API or hosted form; screen for eligibility, duplicates, and fraud blacklists at submit; enrich with carrier and underwriting data; route to the right carrier or agent inside the accept/return window. Premiums, commissions, and reversals post to the double-entry ledger, and every carrier and agency gets its own portal, statements, and dashboards. The AI answers "which carrier converts book X best?" — and can rebalance routing on the spot.

Healthcare

The record: a referral, prior-auth, or provider intake

Intake referrals and authorizations; screen for eligibility and completeness. When a field is missing, a human-in-the-loop secure form — PIN worklist or signed one-record link — lets a coordinator finish it without exposing the whole system. Route to the right provider, network, or facility; track every handoff with error drill-ins and webhook replay. A per-partner knowledge base keeps payer rules searchable, and each client runs on a dedicated single-tenant database built for data isolation and per-partner access control.
